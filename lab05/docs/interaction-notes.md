## Layout & Flow
- Header (“Yatzy”) centered.
- Two main cards: Dice (left), Scorecard (right) — centered as a pair.
- On small screens, cards stack vertically.

## Dice Styling & Interactions (mock-up only)
- Size ≈ 85×85 px, white face, soft pink border (#F4D9E5).
- Hover: lift by 2–3px and subtle pink glow (border → #F472B6).
- (Planned) Held state: fill with #FDE2E4 and keep border pink.

## Buttons
- Primary button is pink (#F472B6), pill-shaped, dark-pink hover (#DB2777).

## Why this works
- Pastel background reduces glare; white cards give structure.
- Pink accents communicate focus/interaction without being harsh.
